How I work

From requirement to a running line

One point of contact from the first conversation through commissioning and support.

  1. 01

    Understand

    The business problem, the line, cycle times and what “done” looks like.

  2. 02

    Design

    Architecture, I/O and PLC memory mapping, operator UI and integration plan.

  3. 03

    Build

    Responsive, asynchronous .NET software with local buffering and logging.

  4. 04

    Integrate

    Cameras, RFID readers, PLCs, instruments, databases, APIs and ERP.

  5. 05

    Test

    On the physical line — calibrating antennas, exposures, sensors and limits.

  6. 06

    Deliver

    Deployment, documentation, reports and alerts, then ongoing support.

Typical engagement: a short discovery call, then a small paid pilot on one station or line, then rollout and support. Scope, timeline and commercial terms are agreed in writing before work starts.

About

Bridging industrial hardware and dependable software.

I’m Raunak Kushwah, an software and automation engineer. I design and build the applications that sit between shop-floor hardware and the business — reading PLCs, cameras, RFID readers, scanners and test instruments, and turning their signals into decisions, operator screens, traceability records and reports.

Factory software can’t afford crashes, frozen screens or missed triggers. I use asynchronous I/O, MVVM architecture and deterministic communication loops, with local caching and background sync to SQL Server and ERP so the line keeps running even when the network doesn’t.

Project types

End-of-line testing, vision inspection, NDT sorting, RFID encoding, FG dispatch checks, Andon and automated reporting.

Industries

Manufacturing assembly lines, automotive fasteners, precision components and gear assemblies.

Integration experience

Siemens, Mitsubishi and Omron PLCs; Zebra RFID; Keyence/Cognex vision; FOERSTER, HBM and ABB equipment; SAP.

Engineering priorities

Hardware interlocks, zero-loss data logging, full traceability and operator-friendly UI.
